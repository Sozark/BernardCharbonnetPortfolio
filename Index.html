Firm Overview

A Unique Combination of Planning & Legal Expertise

Charbonnet & Associates Planners and Consultants, Inc. (CAPC) is a planning and consulting firm whose experience spans local, state, and federal enterprises — including aviation, schools, and municipalities. Founded in 1995 by Bernard L. Charbonnet, Jr., the firm maintains its corporate office in downtown New Orleans with a branch office in St. Louis, Missouri.

Since its inception, CAPC's planners, architects, engineers, and lawyers have delivered consulting projects for public and private entities including Lambert-St. Louis International Airport, Jackson International Airport, Alexandria International Airport, Chicago O'Hare International Airport, Jefferson Parish, the City of Shreveport, the Port of New Orleans, the Orleans Parish School Board, and the Louisiana Community & Technical College System.

What distinguishes CAPC is the ability to provide both planning staff and legal advisors on the same project — affording clients a combination of integrated planning and regulatory counsel unavailable at most firms.

“CAPC consultants consistently bring a high level of professional service to each client and project — combining legal and regulatory consultation with hands-on planning expertise.”

— Firm Mission Statement

Track Record

Selected Projects & Engagements

Decades of proven delivery across aviation, environmental, construction, and public infrastructure — with particular depth in the St. Louis and Louisiana markets.

Flagship Engagement

Lambert-St. Louis International Airport — Expansion & Environmental Program

From 1995–2007, CAPC served on the consulting team providing general advisory services to Lambert-St. Louis International Airport in preparation for its $2.8 billion long-term expansion plan, including the addition of a new 9,000-foot runway. The airport's Environmental Management System received an achievement award in the Large Airport Category by Airports Council International (ACI).

Environmental: Day-to-day expansion program oversight, permitting, monitoring, development of new environmental management programs, and coordination with regulatory agencies.

Mapping & GIS: Mapping of existing airport and airline support facilities into GIS-compatible files for the Master Plan and Part 150 Noise Study.

Privatization Study: Phase I Environmental Site Assessment and Compliance Assessment for the FLY314 Airport Privatization Feasibility Study — a 2,700-acre site.

Social Justice: Facilitation of the recompense program for the Bridgeton, MO community impacted by the new runway corridor.

Flagship Engagement

Louisiana Community & Technical College System — ACT 360 Construction Outreach Program

CAPC works with the LCTCS Facilities Corporation to implement a statewide outreach program that creates an environment affording all small businesses open access to ACT 360 construction projects. The program ensures emerging, small, and underutilized construction-related businesses have viable opportunities to participate across LCTCS facility projects.

Statewide Outreach: Identifying and maintaining a directory of small and disadvantaged businesses; performing outreach to subcontractors and prime contractors.

Small Business Program: Ensuring small, minority-, and women-owned companies are aware of and can participate in all ACT 360 project opportunities.

Technical Assistance: Education seminars covering certification, surety bonding, bidding, insurance, accounting, engineering, and construction law.

Social & Environmental Justice

Equity in Every Project

Charbonnet & Associates is well acquainted with the issues of social and environmental justice — having addressed them directly on past projects including the St. Louis Lambert Airport expansion, the Bridgeton, MO community recompense program, and statewide DBE outreach initiatives.

Social Justice

Giving individuals and groups fair treatment and an impartial share of social, environmental, and economic benefits — promoting fair distribution of advantages and disadvantages within a society regardless of background or status.

Environmental Justice

Concerns the distribution of environmental benefits and burdens people experience at home, at work, or where they play and learn — including access to greenspace, clean air and water, investment in pollution abatement, and protection from industrial, transport-generated, and municipal pollution.

Our DBE Commitment

As a certified Disadvantaged Business Enterprise, CAPC has made the transition from a struggling minority-owned company to an established transportation-consulting firm operating regionally for more than 25 years. We understand what matters to disadvantaged groups — and we deliver.

“We offer and implement solutions.”

  • We do our research when designing community involvement programs, ensuring plans are grounded in real data and local context.

  • We communicate what is at stake and how community input will be used — in the community's own language.

  • We go to them — the community — for input, using visuals to ensure inclusivity for people with varying language skills.

  • We provide education and context — addressing the pros and cons of issues so stakeholders can participate meaningfully.

  • We turn to community leaders to solicit participation and input from the groups most affected by project decisions.

  • We collect and monitor demographic information in real time to allow for adjustments and additions to the plan.

Legal Services

Legal Advisory Services

Through its affiliation with the Law Office of Bernard L. Charbonnet, Jr., CAPC provides an array of professional services combining attorneys, architects, engineers, and environmental consultants who can assist clients across a wide range of endeavors.

Mr. Charbonnet's extensive knowledge of construction law allows CAPC to offer a broader range of services than those normally associated with planning firms. His legal consultants evaluate contracts, advise clients regarding claims made by contractors and subcontractors, and establish preventative procedures to avoid costly litigation.

CAPC has provided consulting or legal services for projects totaling more than three billion dollars in construction — including the $175 million Orleans Parish School Board renovation program, the $650 million New Orleans International Airport expansion, and the $100 million New Orleans East Hospital.

The firm has also represented the Housing Authority of New Orleans (HANO) and currently serves as general counsel to Hospital Service District A in New Orleans.
