Firm Overview

A Unique Combination of Planning & Legal Expertise

Charbonnet & Associates Planners and Consultants, Inc. (CAPC) is a planning and consulting firm whose experience and expertise spans local, state, and federal enterprises — including aviation, schools, and municipalities. Founded in 1995 by Bernard L. Charbonnet, Jr., the firm maintains its corporate office in downtown New Orleans with a branch office in St. Louis, Missouri.

Since its inception, CAPC's planners, architects, engineers, and lawyers have participated in consulting projects for numerous public and private entities including Lambert-St. Louis International Airport, Jackson International Airport, Alexandria International Airport, Chicago O'Hare International Airport, Jefferson Parish Louisiana, the City of Shreveport, Port of New Orleans, Orleans Parish School Board, and the Louisiana Community & Technical College System.

What distinguishes CAPC is the ability to provide both planning staff and legal advisors on the same project — affording clients a unique combination of integrated planning and regulatory counsel unavailable at most firms.

"CAPC consultants consistently bring a high level of professional service to each client and project — combining legal and regulatory consultation with hands-on planning expertise."

— Firm Mission Statement

Lambert-St. Louis International Airport
Expansion & Environmental Program

From 1995–2007, CAPC served on the consulting team providing general advisory services to Lambert-St. Louis International Airport in preparation for its $2.8 billion long-term expansion plan, including the addition of a new 9,000-foot runway. The airport's Environmental Management System received an achievement award in the Large Airport Category by Airports Council International (ACI).

Social Justice: Facilitation of recompense program for Bridgeton, MO community impacted by the new runway corridor

Social & Environmental Justice

Equity in Every Project

Charbonnet & Associates is well acquainted with the issues of social and environmental justice — having addressed these issues directly on past projects including the St. Louis Lambert Airport expansion, the Bridgeton, MO community recompense program, and statewide DBE outreach initiatives.

Social Justice

Giving individuals and groups fair treatment and an impartial share of social, environmental, and economic benefits — promoting fair distribution of advantages and disadvantages within a society regardless of background or status.

Environmental Justice

Concerns the distribution of environmental benefits and burdens people experience at home, at work, or where they play and learn — including access to greenspace, clean air and water, investment in pollution abatement, and protection from industrial, transport-generated, and municipal pollution.

Our DBE Commitment

As a certified Disadvantaged Business Enterprise, CAPC has undergone the transition from a struggling minority-owned company to an established transportation-consulting firm operating regionally for more than 25 years. We understand what matters to disadvantaged groups — and we deliver.

"We offer and implement solutions."

  • We do our research when designing community involvement programs, ensuring plans are grounded in real data and local context.

  • We communicate what is at stake and how community input will be used — in the community's own language.

  • We go to them — the community — for input, using visuals to ensure inclusivity for people with varying language skills.

  • We provide education and context — addressing the pros and cons of issues so stakeholders can participate meaningfully.

  • We turn to community leaders to solicit participation and input from the groups most affected by project decisions.

  • We collect and monitor demographic information in real-time to allow for adjustments and additions to the plan.

Legal Services

Legal Advisory Services

Through its affiliation with the Law Office of Bernard L. Charbonnet, Jr., CAPC provides an array of professional services of unmatched ability — combining attorneys, architects, engineers, and environmental consultants who can assist any client in any endeavor.

Mr. Charbonnet possesses a vast amount of knowledge of construction law allowing CAPC to offer a broader range of services than those normally associated with planning firms.
